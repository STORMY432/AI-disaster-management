FLOOD SAFETY GUIDE

Before a Flood
- Monitor weather forecasts and official alerts.
- Prepare an emergency kit with food, water, flashlight, medicines, and important documents.
- Know evacuation routes.

During a Flood
- Move immediately to higher ground.
- Follow evacuation instructions from authorities.
- Never walk or drive through floodwater.
- Turn off electricity if it is safe to do so.
- Stay away from rivers, drains, and bridges.

After a Flood
- Return home only after authorities declare it safe.
- Drink only clean or boiled water.
- Avoid damaged buildings.
- Watch for electrical hazards.
- Clean and disinfect flooded areas.

Emergency Kit
- Drinking water
- First aid kit
- Torch
- Power bank
- Battery-powered radio
- Dry food
- Medicines
