EARTHQUAKE SAFETY GUIDE

Before an Earthquake
- Secure heavy furniture.
- Prepare an emergency kit.
- Learn safe places in your home.

During an Earthquake
- Drop to the ground.
- Take Cover under a sturdy table.
- Hold On until shaking stops.
- Stay away from windows.
- Do not use elevators.

If Outside
- Stay away from buildings.
- Move to open ground.
- Avoid electric poles.

After an Earthquake
- Check for injuries.
- Turn off gas if leaking.
- Expect aftershocks.
- Listen to official updates.
