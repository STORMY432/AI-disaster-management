Tsunami Safety

Before a Tsunami:
- Learn evacuation routes.
- Monitor official tsunami warnings.
- Prepare an emergency kit.

During a Tsunami:
- Move immediately to higher ground.
- Do not wait to see the wave.
- Stay away from beaches and rivers.
- Follow emergency officials.

After a Tsunami:
- Stay away from flooded areas.
- Avoid damaged roads.
- Wait for official announcements.
- Help injured people if safe.
