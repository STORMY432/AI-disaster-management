WILDFIRE SAFETY GUIDE

Before a Wildfire
- Clear dry leaves around your house.
- Prepare emergency supplies.
- Keep important documents ready.

During a Wildfire
- Evacuate immediately if instructed.
- Wear long sleeves and long pants.
- Cover your nose with an N95 mask or damp cloth.
- Keep windows and doors closed.
- Stay away from smoke.

After a Wildfire
- Return only when authorities allow.
- Avoid burned trees.
- Wear gloves while cleaning.
- Watch for hot spots.
