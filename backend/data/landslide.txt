Landslide Safety

Before a Landslide:
- Watch weather forecasts.
- Avoid building near steep slopes.
- Prepare an emergency kit.

During a Landslide:
- Move away from the landslide path.
- Run to higher ground if possible.
- Protect your head.
- Stay alert for flooding.

After a Landslide:
- Stay away from unstable slopes.
- Report broken utilities.
- Help injured people safely.
- Follow official instructions.
